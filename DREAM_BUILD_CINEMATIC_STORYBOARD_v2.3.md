# SHOSHI + LUNA — Dream Build Cinematic Product Film

## Target
60–75 second premium executive-tech product film. The film must demonstrate real product state and recorded runtime, not fabricated screens.

## Visual Language
Dark obsidian command environment, black/gold SHOSHI, silver/white LUNA, luminous glass dashboards, restrained cinematic motion, premium typography, no noisy cyberpunk clutter. 2D photoreal master visuals remain canonical. Spatial/3D is not required.

## Sequence
### 0–6s — Wake
Black screen. A thin gold pulse appears. The approved Command Center fades in behind SHOSHI and LUNA.
On-screen: `SHOSHI • LUNA / EXECUTIVE AGENCY`.
Voice: “מטרה אחת. שתי מנהלות. מערכת אחת שיודעת להוכיח מה באמת קרה.”

### 6–16s — User Mission
Record a real user command in Hebrew. SHOSHI receives it. Mission Control creates a real mission ID. Executive Weather changes from READY to ACTIVE.

### 16–26s — Executive Split
SHOSHI frames the strategy. LUNA challenges it and proposes an alternative. Council opens with visible session ID, positions and outcome. Avoid fake pre-rendered debate text; use real runtime responses captured from the app.

### 26–38s — Specialist Swarm
Show specialist recruitment: research, competitor intelligence, lead research, proposal, validation. Visualize task flow without implying external work happened unless evidence exists.

### 38–49s — External Network
Show a sandbox external agent: Candidate -> Trusted -> Handshake -> Contact Proposal -> Human Approval -> Send -> Quarantine/Inspection -> Evidence. Briefly flash `PROMPT INJECTION BLOCKED` using an actual test result.

### 49–58s — Voice + Presence
User speaks Hebrew. Transcript appears. SHOSHI answers with her fixed voice. Switch to LUNA and demonstrate the second fixed voice. No voice catalog UI.

### 58–68s — Proof, Not Theater
Evidence Vault and Audit Trail light up. Approval Firewall and Emergency Halt appear as hard controls. On-screen: `FACTS • APPROVAL • EVIDENCE • CONTROL`.

### 68–75s — Executive Brief
Return to the Dream Command Center. Show `Next Best Action`, mission status, Council, external network and evidence. SHOSHI/LUNA remain central.
Final line: `SHOSHI + LUNA — Executive agency that can explain what it knows, what it did, and what still needs your approval.`

## Film Acceptance Rules
- Runtime capture only for claims of working functionality.
- No mock external-success screens.
- No fake payment/revenue claims.
- No generic avatars.
- No 3D dependency.
- Hebrew captions and speech must be natural and readable.
- Capture at stable frame rate and crop out development/debug chrome.
